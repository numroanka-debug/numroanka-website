Why Chaldean numerology?

The name Numro Anka comes from our connection with numerology and numbers. “Numro” comes from the word numerology while “Anka” (अंक) means number in Sanskrit and Hindi.

At Numro Anka we follow the Chaldean tradition of numerology which has its roots in ancient Babylon. Chaldean numerology is well known for name analysis where letters are given numerical values and these numbers are studied to understand the patterns connected with a name.

Our approach goes beyond looking at numbers in isolation. For a deeper and more detailed analysis we also consider the astrological chart when the required birth details are available. This allows us to study the relationship between the numbers in a name and the individual’s astrological influences.

For us a name is more than just a set of letters. It has its own numerical pattern and significance. Through Chaldean numerology and our deeper analysis we look at the connection between letters, numbers and astrology to help people understand and choose names for themselves, their businesses and their brands.

Our system

Chaldean (c. 4000 BCE)

Letters are valued by vibration, not by alphabet position. The scale runs 1 to 8. The number 9 is held sacred and never assigned to a letter because nine was understood to contain all the others. The name number is read alongside the birth date, which is why Chaldean numerology became the tradition of choice for business & name analysis across India.

The other system

Pythagorean (c. 600 BCE)

Simpler and more common online. Letters are numbered by position, A=1 through I=9, then repeating. It is a fine system for casual readings but it flattens the vibrational distinctions that make Chaldean name analysis so precise. For example C, G, L and S all sounding alike and all carrying 3.

How it works

What we calculate, and how

Life Path — from your birthday

Every digit of your date of birth is summed and reduced to a single digit (or a master number 11, 22, 33). It is your central lesson — the road, not the vehicle.

Destiny & Soul Urge — from your name

All letters of your birth name give the Destiny (what you must become); vowels alone give the Soul Urge (what you privately want); consonants give the Personality (the mask the world meets).

Cycles — from today's date

FAQ

Questions readers ask

Which name should I use — birth name or current name?

Your full name exactly as printed on your birth certificate. That is the vibration you arrived with. A changed name (marriage, spelling correction, pen name) adds an overlay worth analysing separately — which is exactly what the name-correction section of a premium report examines.

What are master numbers 11, 22 and 33?

When a reduction lands on 11, 22 or 33, tradition holds that the number should not be reduced further. These are "master" vibrations — higher-octave versions of 2, 4 and 6 — carrying greater potential and, honestly, greater nervous strain. Our calculator preserves them automatically.

Is my data stored or shared anywhere?

No. Every calculation on Numro Anka runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or sold — you can verify this yourself: the site works even with your internet disconnected after loading.

Is numerology scientific?

No — and we will never pretend otherwise. Numerology is a symbolic tradition, thousands of years old, used for reflection, self-understanding and fun. Treat it the way you treat astrology or tarot: a mirror for thinking about your life, not a substitute for medical, financial or psychological advice.

Why does my Chaldean result differ from another site's?

Most websites default to the Pythagorean system, where letters are valued by alphabet position. Numro Anka uses the older Chaldean values (A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=8…). Both are legitimate traditions — but they will often give different numbers for the same name.

Can numerology really improve my business name?

That is the tradition's claim, not a scientific finding. What we can say: for a century, Indian businesses from film studios to conglomerates have chosen names on Chaldean vibrations — typically aiming for the money numbers 6, 9 or the powerhouse 8. Whether it works or works through confidence is yours to decide. If it interests you, our free calculator accepts any name you care to test.
