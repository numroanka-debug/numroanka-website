The Core 5 · Psychological Numerology

Five numbers. One complete psychology of you.

This is the framework American numerology built its industry on: Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, Personality and Birthday — the road you walk, the talent you carry, the heart you hide, the face you show, and the gift you arrived with. Read separately, they inform. Read together, they explain.

I

Life Path — the road

From your date of birth. The central lesson of this lifetime and the direction everything else serves. The one number to know if you only know one.

II

Expression — the talent

From your full birth name (also called Destiny). What you are actually built to become — the capabilities you came equipped with, whether or not you've used them yet.

III

Soul Urge — the heart

From the vowels of your name. What you privately want, beneath duty and persona. Ignore it long enough and even success feels hollow.

IV

Personality — the doorway

From the consonants. The impression that walks into the room before you do — the filter through which strangers interpret everything else about you.

V

Birthday — the gift

From the day you were born. A specific, reliable talent available at any moment — the thing you can lean on even when the rest of the chart is under weather.

Σ

Together — the reading

The five numbers answer each other. A heart that fights the road, a mask that undersells the talent — the relationships between your numbers are where the real insight lives.

A word on how to use this

The Core 5 is a framework for self-reflection, not a psychological assessment. Its questions are good — what do you want, what do you show, what are you built for — and the numbers give them structure. Read it the way you'd read a good prompt in a journal, and take what's useful.
