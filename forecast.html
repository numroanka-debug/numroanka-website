How to use these forecasts

A personal year shapes the season, the month sets the job, the day suggests the mood — none of it overrides your judgement. Use the calendar the way farmers use almanacs: dates worth favouring, not commands to obey. And if a launch-month arrives and the product isn't ready, ship in the next 1-month — the cycle forgives.
